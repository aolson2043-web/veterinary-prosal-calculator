Veterinary Compensation Offer Checklist

Before comparing two offers, make sure you understand the compensation formula behind each one.

Compensation terms to identify

  1. Base salary or guarantee: What amount is guaranteed?
  2. Production percentage: What percentage applies, and does it change at different thresholds?
  3. Eligible production: What services or products count?
  4. Reconciliation period: Monthly, quarterly, annual, or another schedule?
  5. Negative accrual: Can a shortfall carry into the next period?
  6. Deductions and exclusions: Are discounts, refunds, lab costs, or other items treated differently?
  7. Payment timing: When is production compensation actually paid?

Questions worth asking before you sign

Can you provide a sample production report?
How is eligible production calculated in the practice-management system?
How often can I review my production numbers?
How are corrections, refunds, or late payments handled?
Is there negative accrual, and when does it reset?

Use the calculator before comparing offers

Enter each offer's guarantee, production percentage, and expected eligible production separately. That gives you a consistent way to compare the compensation formula before considering benefits and other non-cash terms.
