Veterinary ProSal & production-pay questions

Compensation agreements vary, so use your own contract language as the controlling source.

What is ProSal?

“ProSal” is commonly used to describe compensation that combines a salary guarantee with production-based compensation. The exact calculation and reconciliation schedule depend on the agreement.

What counts as production?

That varies by employer and contract. Some plans exclude or adjust certain products, discounts, refunds, fees, or services. Enter only the production your agreement says is eligible.

What is negative accrual?

In plans with negative accrual, a production shortfall from one period may carry into a future period before additional production compensation is earned. Not every plan uses it.
