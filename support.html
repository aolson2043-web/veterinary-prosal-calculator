Frequently asked questions

How does Check Pay work?

Enter your compensation plan, production, and employer-reported gross earnings. SalaryNspect calculates expected pay based on the details you provide and compares the results.

What is negative accrual?

If your plan permits deficits to carry forward, SalaryNspect can track unrecovered balances between periods and account for them when calculating later earnings. Your contract controls whether negative accrual applies.

What's the difference between Original Audit and Current Report?

Original Audit preserves the original saved comparison. Current Report can reflect later adjustments to your pay and production information.

How many reports can I save for free?

You can save three comparisons for free. A one-time SalaryNspect Pro purchase unlocks additional comparisons and detailed reporting.

Why does a PDF export ask for Pro?

Original Audit PDF exports are free. Current Report and detailed PDF exports are Pro features.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the app's Pro purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase.

Does SalaryNspect require an account?

No. SalaryNspect is designed to keep your compensation records on your device without requiring a SalaryNspect account.

Are these official payroll calculations?

No. SalaryNspect provides estimates based on your inputs. Verify discrepancies against your signed compensation agreement and official employer payroll records.
