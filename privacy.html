Website privacy

The free calculators on this website perform calculations locally in your browser. We do not intentionally transmit the compensation figures you enter to our servers.

We use Google Analytics to understand website usage, including page visits and interactions such as calculator usage and App Store link clicks. Google may process technical information in accordance with its privacy policies. You can use browser privacy controls to limit tracking.

Clicking an App Store link takes you to Apple, which operates under its own privacy terms. The SalaryNspect iPhone app is designed to store your compensation data on your device without requiring an account.

For privacy questions contact support@salarynspect.com.
