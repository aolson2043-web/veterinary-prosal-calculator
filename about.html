About SalaryNspect

Know what you should be paid before payday.

Why it exists

Production, commission and variable-pay plans can be difficult to follow from one pay period to the next. SalaryNspect was created as an independent iPhone app to make compensation easier to understand, document and compare.

The app was built by a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and active first responder through AO Digital Labs. It is not operated by a payroll company or compensation advisory firm.

SalaryNspect helps you model your plan, track production, check expected versus reported gross pay, and compare offers. Your information stays on your device; no account is required.
